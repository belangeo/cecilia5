BucketBrigadeDevice : Cross-fed bucket-brigade delay synthesis¶

Description¶

Based on pyo’s 08_bucket_brigade_device.py example, with the regular Cecilia sampler replacing the input oscillator. Two delay lines feed themselves and each other through ring modulators and lowpass filters. Feedback sets self-feedback; Cross Feedback sets feedback from the opposite branch. Lowpass Frequency controls both filters. The output is compressed with threshold -30 dB, ratio 4, and knee 0.5, matching the example.

Delay Time 1 and 2 set the center delay times. Slow sine modulation adds 0.05 and 0.08 seconds at 0.005 and 0.003 Hz respectively. Ring Frequency 1 and 2 set the center ring-modulator frequencies, with drift of 50 and 40 Hz at 0.007 and 0.008 Hz. Delay times are limited to one sample through 2 seconds; ring frequencies are limited to 1 through 5000 Hz after modulation.

The sampler input is scaled by 0.025 before entering the feedback network. The two filtered branches alternate across output channels; mono output averages both.

Input¶

The standard Cecilia sampler provides sound-file or live input, with its normal looping, pitch, and polyphony controls. In the routing view, this module can also process audio from upstream modules.

Parameter jitter¶

Each parameter has independent smooth random jitter. Amplitudes are relative: parameter * random(1 - amplitude, 1 + amplitude), clipped to the parameter range.

Each jitter uses a pair of half-width amplitude and frequency sliders. Amplitude sliders are logarithmic, start at 0.0001, and use gliss=0.0 so their values apply immediately at initialization. Frequency sliders set the jitter rate in Hz.

Graph Only¶

Overall Amplitude controls the output amplitude over the performance duration.

Popups & Toggles¶

Polyphony Voices sets the number of simultaneous sampler voices.

Polyphony Spread sets the pitch relationship between voices. Both polyphony controls are set at initialization.
