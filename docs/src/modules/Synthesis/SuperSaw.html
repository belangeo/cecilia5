SuperSaw : Seven detuned sawtooth oscillators¶

Description¶

Based on pyo’s SuperSaw emulation of the Roland JP-8000 Supersaw. Frequency sets the central pitch, Detune spreads six side oscillators around the central oscillator, and Oscillator Balance controls their relative levels. At balance zero, only the central oscillator is heard.

This module generates its own audio and requires no input sound file. In the routing view, it is a source module.

Parameter jitter¶

Frequency, detune, and oscillator balance have independent smooth random jitter. Frequency jitter is relative: frequency * random(1 - amplitude, 1 + amplitude). Detune and balance jitter are additive offsets between -amplitude and +amplitude. The resulting base frequency stays within 20 to 20000 Hz; detune and balance stay within 0 to 1. Polyphony transposition follows frequency jitter.

Each jitter uses a pair of half-width amplitude and frequency sliders. Amplitude sliders are logarithmic, start at 0.0001, and use gliss=0.0 so their values apply immediately at initialization. Frequency sliders set the jitter rate in Hz.

Graph Only¶

Overall Amplitude controls the output amplitude over the performance duration.

Popups & Toggles¶

Polyphony Voices sets the number of simultaneous synthesis voices.

Polyphony Spread sets the pitch relationship between voices. Both polyphony controls are set at initialization.
